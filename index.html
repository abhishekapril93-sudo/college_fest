Welcome to TechFest 2027

TechFest 2027 is the annual inter-college technology festival organized by the Department of Computer Science. Join us for three exciting days of competitions, workshops, and tech talks that bring together the brightest minds from colleges across the country.

Dates & Venue

  • Dates: March 14 - 16, 2027
  • Venue: Main Auditorium & CS Block, Quad AI School of Technology, Patna
  • Time: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM daily
